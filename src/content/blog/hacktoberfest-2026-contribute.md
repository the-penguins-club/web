October is Hacktoberfest, the month when people around the world send their first (or fiftieth) contribution to open source projects. The Penguins Club is taking part, and our whole GitHub organization is open for contributions.

You do not need to be an expert. Fixing a typo, translating a page, or improving a confusing README all count as real contributions to us.

## Where to contribute

Our repositories live at [github.com/the-penguins-club](https://github.com/the-penguins-club). Good places to start:

- **bd-startups**: a community-maintained list of startups in Bangladesh. Add a missing company or fix outdated details.
- **Awesome-Cybersecurity**: a curated list of security resources. Suggest a tool, course, or write-up that helped you.
- **uradhura**: open issues are a good way to get to know the codebase.
- **The website**: this site is open source too, in [The-Penguins-Club/web](https://github.com/The-Penguins-Club/web). Improvements to pages, design and accessibility are welcome.

## Making your first pull request

1. Pick a repository and read its README.
2. Look through the open issues, or open a new one describing what you want to change.
3. Fork the repository, make your change on a branch, and open a pull request.
4. A maintainer will review it and may ask for small changes. That is normal.

## Check the official rules

Hacktoberfest has its own rules about which pull requests count towards your progress. Read them at [hacktoberfest.com](https://hacktoberfest.com/) before you start, so none of your work is wasted.

## Stuck? Ask us

Join our [Telegram group](https://t.me/penguinsclubnetwork) and tell us what you are trying to do. Members who have been through it will help you get unstuck. If you would like to collaborate with us as a person or an organization, email [uthsob@thepenguins.club](mailto:uthsob@thepenguins.club).
